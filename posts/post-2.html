What makes glioblastoma difficult to treat?

Science note · Starter article

Glioblastoma combines invasive growth, cellular diversity, and a tumor microenvironment that can limit therapy.

Glioblastoma is an aggressive brain tumor with substantial variation between cells within the same tumor. Some tumor cells infiltrate surrounding brain tissue, making complete surgical removal difficult. The tumor microenvironment can also suppress immune responses, while treatment-resistant cell states may survive standard therapy. These challenges motivate research into combinations that address both tumor-cell biology and antitumor immunity.
