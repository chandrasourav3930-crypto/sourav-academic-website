Why antimicrobial resistance is a One Health problem

Science note · Starter article

Antimicrobial resistance connects human health, animal health, food systems, and the environment.

Antimicrobial resistance can emerge and spread across connected human, animal, and environmental settings. Antibiotic use creates selection pressure, while movement of people, animals, food, water, and bacteria can move resistant organisms or resistance genes between settings. A One Health approach combines surveillance, responsible antimicrobial use, infection prevention, and environmental management rather than treating each sector in isolation.
