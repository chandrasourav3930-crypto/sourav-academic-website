CRISPR: a programmable tool for biological research

Science note · Starter article

CRISPR-based systems let researchers target particular DNA sequences to study gene function and more.

CRISPR-based tools use guide molecules to direct a protein, such as Cas9, to a selected nucleic-acid sequence. Depending on the system, researchers can disrupt a gene, alter a sequence, or regulate gene expression. Careful controls are essential: editing efficiency varies, unintended changes are possible, and observed phenotypes should be validated using independent methods.
